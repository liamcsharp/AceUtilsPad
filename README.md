# AceUtilsPad: The Evolution

Version 1.2.0 is the largest update to AceUtilsPad so far. It adds two new menu shapes, a built-in camera mod, a full widget system, and a visual overhaul.

## Highlights

### Menu shapes
- **Wrist Menu**: new 3D body with a side grip, positioned beside your hand instead of over it. The back is now solid.
- **Orb Menu**: a grabbable orb you throw to deploy the menu in the world, with curved side panels.
- **AceOne Phone**: the menu as a smartphone with a home screen of apps.
- New Tiny and Mini sizes.

### AceCam
- Grabbable camera tablet with live preview.
- Modes: first person, third person, follow, tablet, selfie, orbit, free.
- Sliders for FOV, smoothness, clipping, distance, tablet size and move speed.
- Seven presets, rotation and movement controls, reset.
- 1080p PNG photos with an on-tablet gallery.
- Cinematic mode and 720p AVI video recording (no audio).

### AceWidgets
- 13 built-in widgets plus a Widget Dock.
- Drag, resize, rotate, recolor, lock, group and minimize.
- Pin to your view, the world, or your hand.
- Six layouts, unlimited saved profiles, share codes.
- Widget Studio for building custom widgets from safe building blocks.
- Per-widget alert rules.

### Look and feel
- Eight themes: Lava, Sakura, Forest, Canyon, Cave, Beach, City, Clouds.
- Open, close, press, ripple and page-slide animations.
- Welcome screen on startup.

### Players and lobbies
- Custom nametags: platform icon, ping dot, FPS, account age, friend star, notes, self tags.
- Ban-risk labels in Mod Detection.
- Owner and friend join alerts with selectable sounds.

### Media
- Music player reads the current song and timestamp from Windows. QuickSong.exe is no longer needed.

### Performance and PC
- Anti-lag mode (Off / Auto / Always).
- Reduced menu redraw cost and smoother hand tracking.
- Mouse hover and click, scroll wheel, keyboard shortcuts and typing on PC.

## Removed
- Custom props
- Old themes and seasonal auto-switch
- QuickSong.exe

## Installation
1. Install BepInEx for Gorilla Tag.
2. Drop `AceUtilsPad.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins`.
3. Launch the game. Press the left Y button in VR or J on PC to open the menu.

## Notes
- Ban-risk labels are estimates. Use mods in private lobbies when unsure.
- Widget sharing works through share codes; there is no online marketplace.
